To renew a driver's licence, book a slot on the agency portal at https://fixture-roads.gov.ng/portal, bring the old licence and a passport photograph, and pay the renewal fee of 12,500 naira.

A renewed licence is valid for three years. A person whose licence expired more than six months ago also pays a late fee of 5,000 naira.
